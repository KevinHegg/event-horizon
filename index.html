EVENT HORIZON

Hold back the collapse of the galaxy.

  1. Swipe through glowing tethers to harvest dark-energy orbs.
  2. Tap an orb to stabilize it briefly.
  3. Tap streaking stars for a bonus.
  4. Keep the Dark Energy meter alive.
  5. When the meter empties, the galaxy collapses.

The black hole always wins.
Your job is to delay it.
